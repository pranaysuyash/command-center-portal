# Portal scenario authority remediation

The portal mesh messages do not carry a reviewed shipment identifier and revision. They are now recorded as scenario information only and cannot auto-complete, reject or override the preset lifecycle. HOS events, including older boolean-only and newer assessment payloads, are not driver-log or legal authority. The independent shared shipment workspace remains a separate workflow; this change does not bind mesh events to it. Previously persisted scenario decisions are preserved and not retroactively verified.

The exception tower identifies the two baseline entries as examples and counts mesh messages separately as unverified. The print manifest's local scenario review does not generate shipper or driver signatures or tender acceptance, and its notice remains present after review. EDI examples are local artifacts, not sent transactions: the 990 requires an explicit accept or decline choice, resets that choice for another order, and decline output no longer includes an acceptance sentence or a confirmed rate. Carrier identity and contents remain synthetic and no EDI conformance claim follows from generation.

Regression tests cover legacy and modeled HOS message descriptions, missing/declined 990 responses, sample exception labeling, and the server-rendered print signature placeholders. The cross-product fixture expects unknown telemetry health without supplied DTCs. Browser event handling, persisted prior scenarios, real signatures, transport EDI, mobile, keyboard and user acceptance require independent testing. `npm test`: 6 files, 34 tests passed; `npm run typecheck` passed. These are local automated checks, not operational validation.
